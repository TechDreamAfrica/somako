About Soma Ko Ghana

Ghana's premier all-in-one marketplace platform connecting people with the services they need.

Our Story

Soma Ko was founded with a simple mission: to make everyday services accessible to everyone in Ghana. We recognized the challenges people face in finding reliable services - from ordering food and medicine to renting equipment and booking rides.

Our platform brings together all these essential services in one convenient place, making life easier for millions of Ghanaians. Whether you're in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, or anywhere across the country, Soma Ko is your trusted partner for all your daily needs.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of customers and partner with hundreds of local businesses, restaurants, pharmacies, and service providers who share our commitment to quality and reliability.

Our Mission

To empower Ghanaians by providing seamless access to essential services through technology, connecting communities with local businesses and service providers they can trust.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading marketplace platform, transforming how people access services and supporting the growth of local economies across the continent.
